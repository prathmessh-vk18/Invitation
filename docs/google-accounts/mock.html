A clickable mock of Google sign-in. Switch between what a couple sees on their phone and what you see in admin. All names and numbers are examples.

Tap the buttons inside the screens too.

Ordered before sign-in existed

A couple whose invite went live earlier signs in for the first time, from the banner on their order page or the site's Sign in link.

  • If the Google email matches the email on their order, the invite is found automatically.
  • Otherwise “Find my order” links it once, with the invite link, order number and phone.
  • RSVPs count from the day this launches. Earlier replies went straight to their WhatsApp, and the dashboard says so.
  • Nothing changes for them if they never sign in: their invite, order page and edit link work as today.

The nudge, mid-form

After the couple finishes their names and starts the functions, a card offers to save their progress. It never blocks the form and can be dismissed.

  • Signing in keeps them on the same step.
  • The draft then follows them to any phone or laptop.
  • We get their email, so we can remind them to finish.

First visit, nothing yet

Someone who signs in directly, with no draft or order. One job: show what they get, then get them started.

  • If their Google email matches an old order, they skip this and see their invite.
  • The example panel sells RSVP tracking, clearly marked as an example.
  • “Find my order” links an order placed with another email.

Came back with a draft

The unfinished invite moves to the top with exactly where they stopped. Everything else stays below.

  • “Continue” opens the form at step 4.
  • The preview shows their own names and design so far.

Invite is live

Their own invite and live RSVP counts replace the example. This is the screen couples will come back to.

  • Share and copy the link in one tap.
  • RSVPs by function, the guest list, and an Excel download.
  • Edits go through the existing “Ask for changes”.
