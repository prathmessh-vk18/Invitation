# Illustration credits

Original illustrations generated for Aavjoo with an AI image model (via Figma, October 2026).
No existing artwork was referenced or copied. Full-size source PNGs are in `/art-source/illus`; the site uses the JPEGs.

`haveli-*` (October 2026) were generated the same way, as original paintings in the style of a Rajasthani
palace gatefold card a couple shared with us; nothing from that card was traced or copied.
`haveli-monogram.jpg` is the couple's own A&V monogram, used with their permission.

`wardrobe-*` (October 2026) are four couple-outfit paintings for Akshay & Vidhi's Wardrobe section, generated the same way, with the white background removed.

`shiv-*` (November 2026) are original paintings for the Gauri Shankar design, generated the same way: Shiv's palm and
Parvati's foot as separate layers (white background removed), an empty dawn sky, Shiv and Parvati seated on Kailash seen
from behind, and the meeting composed whole for share previews. The design's idea (Shiv's hand receiving Parvati's foot)
is a traditional vivah motif; no existing card's artwork was traced or copied.
